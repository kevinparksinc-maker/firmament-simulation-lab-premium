## Interpretation

**Why the Iron Bowl matters:** Repeating the same directional error over three successive annual meetings suggests a possible stable bias from fixed configuration, side assignment, or recurring seasonal sky patterns. Review scores, evidence traces, and favorite assignment game by game. It is not a valid reason to reverse picks without an independent test set.

**Why the 20-game test and 7-game test differ:** The former mixed college football, baseball and championship fixtures, while this trial uses only recurring college football rivalry matchups. Neither trial is large or historically audited enough to demonstrate predictive edge. Comparisons across these distinct samples should not be treated as controlled experimental evidence.

**Synthesis:** A 50/50 numerical fusion of the two frames produced no improvement over either frame in this sample. Its output is a third derived prediction, but not a separate astronomical measurement or demonstrated independent signal.

## Data-quality constraints and research eligibility

1. The data contains specific UTC kickoff timestamps and source URLs. Only the 2023 Iron Bowl uses an observed kickoff time (14:40 local); other times are documented **scheduled times**, potentially a few minutes different from observed starts.
2. Favorite/underdog assignment is included for all seven games, but some individual odds sources and closing lines still require independent audit. Do not report this as a certified favorite-baseline comparison.
3. The sample includes only seven events from three rivalries; it does not fulfill the full Division I annual-rivalry census or 20-year historical window.
4. Match outcomes are known to the evaluator; coefficients were not fit to this sample. Future tests must prevent post-result leakage and keep fixtures chronological.
5. Rivalry annual meetings do not occur at identical clock times, always the same stadium, or necessarily each calendar year. Use historical event-specific coordinates, local time zone and actual kickoff whenever available.
6. The experimental method designs have not been scientifically validated as predictors of sports outcomes. Do not make wagering claims from this dataset.

## Required next expansion

- Acquire and verify year-by-year meetings across a wide register: Michigan–Ohio State, Alabama–Auburn, Texas–Oklahoma, Army–Navy, Georgia–Florida, Ole Miss–Mississippi State, Clemson–South Carolina, Oregon–Oregon State, USC–Notre Dame, and basketball rivalries tracked separately.
- Import actual kickoff and venue from primary university/league box scores; store local timestamp, UTC timestamp, timezone, source URL and provenance.
- Import source-linked pregame/closing odds and line timestamps independently of outcomes. Place verified favorite on Ascendant.
- Add automated duplicates, chronology, incomplete field quarantine and house/planet evidence checks.
- Run a 100+ rivalry-game pilot and then a larger multi-season test with separate train/validation years and per-rivalry segmentation.
- Compare God/Agent/Synthesis, individual 21 methods, favorite-only baseline, hit rate on directional calls and no-call coverage, with uncertainty intervals.
