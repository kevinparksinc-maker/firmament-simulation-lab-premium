## What this experiment demonstrates

1. **Ascendant reversal materially changes assignments and raw A/B scores.** It affected 16 Agent calculations and changed 12 raw A/B leader categories in this fixture. The fixed-wheel God values matched across paired calls.
2. **Correcting the horizon alone does not imply the system predicts Douglas.** Many corrected combined methods tie or have unavailable inputs; the seven raw B leads include methods with different evidence and certification states.
3. **God/Agent cancellation must be investigated.** Combined equal raw readings jumped from five to fifteen; before treating these as informative ties, inspect whether symmetric 180° house-family outcomes cancel when averaged.
4. **CORE_21 caveat:** the upstream KP dependency still calculates from the corrected engine. An ascendant-only 180° control reconstructs the intended west horizon for method chart geometry, but does not byte-for-byte reproduce the old KP implementation. Some other coupled sublayers may also differ from archived pre-fix code.
5. **Traditional astrological method certification is separate.** This runs code but cannot prove the method is doctrinally complete or that astrology predicts the result.
6. **Start time uncertainty:** contemporary sources differ slightly (noon vs 12:30 pm scheduled); the exact first bell is not established to the minute. Re-run nearby timestamps and perform properly sourced independent samples before judging a method.
